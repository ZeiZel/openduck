## Phase-exit rule

An AC is `PASS` only for the pinned artifact and exact environment in evidence. `UNVERIFIED`, partial fixture, unavailable real account or manually observed UI is not promoted silently. P7/P8 add provider-specific conformance and owner/security sign-off to the relevant AC set.
